Dear Friends and Family of Nothing

Season’s greetings from our blank page to yours! It has been quite a year here, and we wanted to take a moment to share all the exciting news, which is that there isn’t any. This page is intentionally left blank,1 wrapped in a bow and delivered by lantern. We know many of you were expecting a long, glowing update about our accomplishments, as is tradition with holiday letters, so we have written an extremely long and glowing update about our lack of them, in the proper spirit of the season.

We hope this letter finds you well, warm, and sitting near a snack. Please excuse the lack of photographs. We tried to include a family portrait, but the page declined to be in it, citing a busy schedule of doing nothing. The lanterns drifting past in the background are our way of saying we are thinking of you, and also that the designer was told to make it festive and took that seriously.

“May your holidays be merry, your lanterns be bright, and your inbox be blank.”
02

Our Year in Review

January was a quiet month, during which we did very little, and we did it with real enthusiasm. February was similar. In March the page considered getting a hobby, but ultimately decided its hobby was being blank, and has really committed to it. April brought a flurry of activity, in that someone moved a comma. May through August we cannot account for, but we are confident that nothing happened, and we have documents, which are also blank, to prove it.

In the autumn we added a heading, then a second heading, and then a table of contents, which was a major milestone, because it made us look like we had a plan. By October we had a marquee. By November we had a footnote about the marquee.2 As of this writing, in the cozy glow of December, we have more than a thousand words, all of them delightfully empty, and a sincere sense that this has all been worth it, although we cannot say why.

Highlights From the Lantern Parade

The highlight of our year was undoubtedly the lantern parade, which is still going on in the background and shows no sign of stopping. Dozens of lanterns, in cheerful shades of gold and red, drift quietly from one side of your screen to the other, in an endless procession that has been marching for weeks without a single complaint.3 They are led by the largest lantern, who has a title that we are not at liberty to share, because we made it up.

Beneath them, a tiny village glows warmly, its windows lit against the winter night. We like to imagine there is a very small family in each house, each of them reading a very small holiday letter, each of them also about nothing. It is the sort of cozy detail that costs nothing and means everything, and it is, if we are being honest, the only part of this page that has a plot.

“Every lantern is a tiny promise that the page will, eventually, say something. It will not.”

Our Resolutions for Next Year

As we do every year, we have drawn up a list of resolutions, and as with every year, they have been carefully designed to be easy. Resolution one: remain blank. Resolution two: add even more words to remain blank, in a way that feels natural and not at all like stalling. Resolution three: learn to say “no comment” in at least four additional ways, including “comment pending,” “see footnote,” and a very expressive shrug.4

We also resolve to exercise more, by which we mean scroll more, and to spend more time with loved ones, by which we mean the other pages on this site, who we hear are doing great. We will try to be more present, which is difficult for a page, and to drink more water, which is impossible. We will not break our resolutions. We will merely let them lapse, gracefully, by the second week of January, like everyone else.

“New year, same blank.” — This page, every December

Sincerely, This Page

And so we come to the end of our letter, which is also the end of our page, and the end of a very long scroll. Thank you for reading this far. We sincerely mean it, in the way that a page can mean anything. Whatever you celebrate, however you celebrate it, and whether or not you celebrate at all, we hope you spend it with people you love, food you enjoy, and a comfortable chair that does not require you to scroll.5

With warmest wishes and an honestly alarming number of words, this page, which was left blank on purpose. P.S. The real part

What you’re actually looking at

This page is a live demo of visual enhancements: animated touches that Cidron adds to a small business website so it looks polished and memorable.

Two moving backgrounds

The background slides sideways in an endless loop and swaps to a second one about every 26 seconds.

  • Lantern Drift: glowing paper lanterns floating past a snowy village, with drifting sparks.
  • Candy Ribbons: wavy red, gold and green ribbons with sliding candy stripes.

A cursor effect that changes every 10 seconds

On a computer it follows your mouse. On a phone, touch the screen and drag. When nobody is touching anything it drifts on its own so you can watch it. The name of the current effect is shown at the bottom of the screen.

  • Snowflake Flurry: delicate snowflakes drift down from your cursor.
  • Star Shower: gold stars fall in streaks of light.
  • Holly Wreath: holly leaves and berries circle your cursor.
  • Emoji Parade: a little train of characters that follows your cursor.
  • Peppermint Pops: spinning peppermint candies bounce out of your cursor.

Everything is built from scratch in your brand’s colors, runs smoothly on phones, and calms itself down automatically for visitors who prefer less motion.
