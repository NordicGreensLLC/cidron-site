Welcome to the Lounge (Shoes Optional)

Welcome. You have arrived at the quietest corner of the internet, a calming space designed to be completely, deeply, intentionally blank.1 Please leave your worries at the door. There is a little basket for them. The basket is also blank. We have dimmed the lights, which is why the sky above you appears to be doing something dramatic in green. That is not content. That is ambience, and ambience is free, unlike the content you are not receiving.

Our staff has been trained to say nothing with great warmth. If you need anything at all, please don’t hesitate to not ask. Everything on this page has been arranged to feel like a spa lobby: soft, spacious, slightly expensive-looking, and aggressively without a reception desk. If you notice that you are relaxed, that is because there is nothing here to be stressed about. If you notice that you are bored, that is also because there is nothing here, but we prefer the first interpretation.

“In the beginning there was whitespace, and the whitespace was good.”
02

Guided Meditation on a Blank Page

Let us begin. Sit comfortably, or scroll comfortably, whichever is nearer. Take a deep breath in through your nose and consider this blank page. Now breathe out through your mouth and consider it again. Notice how it has not changed. Notice how this is, in fact, the entire point. A blank page asks nothing of you, expects nothing from you, and will never text you at a quarter to midnight to ask whether you saw its email.5

Imagine a field of tall grass, or a field of tall whitespace, whichever is easier to picture. In the distance, a single paragraph walks toward you, and you realize it is this one. Do not be afraid. It means nothing by it. Now let each word float past you like a leaf on a slow river, and notice that the river keeps moving sideways, steadily, and that you are somehow not getting anywhere either. That is not a bug. That is the whole practice.

The Aurora, Explained Vaguely

People often ask what those glowing ribbons are doing in the sky behind this text. The scientific answer is that charged particles from the sun collide with gases in the upper atmosphere. The honest answer is that we asked them to make the page look nicer and they said yes.2 The ribbons slide gently sideways forever, which scientists call “a lot of work for something that does not appear in the budget.”

We could tell you more, but we would be making it up, and this is a page about nothing, not a page about making things up, which is a different page that we also have. If you are wondering whether the green is real green: it is. If you are wondering whether the pink is real pink: also yes. If you are wondering whether any of this will help you finish your taxes: no, and we apologize, and we have added a small, calming sparkle to your cursor to make up for it.

“Everything here is optional, including this sentence.”

Complimentary Beverage Menu

Our beverage menu is intentionally blank, but we are delighted to describe it in detail. For the traveler in need of warmth, we offer a hot cup of nothing, steeped for exactly zero minutes. For those who like it cold, there is an iced nothing, served in a glass the size of your expectations.4 A sparkling nothing is available on Fridays. All drinks are prepared by a barista who has been silent since 2019 and is, frankly, very good at it.3

Please note that substitutions are welcome. You may swap the nothing for another nothing at no additional charge, and oat milk is not available because there is no milk, oat or otherwise. A tip jar sits near the exit. It contains no tips, because we do not accept tips, because we are a web page. Thank you for your understanding, and for continuing to scroll through our lounge.

“The tea is blank. The tea is excellent.”

Check-Out Instructions

Your stay is almost over. We hope you feel rested, refreshed, and mildly suspicious that you just read eleven hundred words about nothing. That is the correct response. Check-out is whenever you like, and you will not be charged for the nothing, the ambience, or the sparkles that followed you around. Please remember to take all your belongings with you, such as your phone, your keys, and whatever sense of time you had before you started reading.7

Before you go, we invite you to review your experience. Five stars is a lovely number, and so is zero, which is the number of items on this page. If you would like to take a piece of the lounge home with you, keep scrolling to the part that is not blank, where we explain how to get the same calming, glowing, gently moving effects on your own business website.6

The real part

What you’re actually looking at

This page is a live demo of visual enhancements: animated touches that Cidron adds to a small business website so it looks polished and memorable.

Two moving backgrounds

The background slides sideways in an endless loop and swaps to a second one about every 26 seconds.

  • Aurora Ribbons: soft ribbons of green, blue and violet light that ripple and slide across a night sky.
  • Drifting Starfield: three layers of stars, a ringed planet and shooting stars gliding past in an endless loop.

A cursor effect that changes every 10 seconds

On a computer it follows your mouse. On a phone, touch the screen and drag. When nobody is touching anything it drifts on its own so you can watch it. The name of the current effect is shown at the bottom of the screen.

  • Glowing Orb: a soft ball of light that drifts after your cursor.
  • Sparkle Dust: twinkling stars that scatter wherever you move.
  • Constellation: drifting points that connect with fine lines around your cursor.
  • Ink Trail: a flowing ribbon of ink that thickens when you slow down.
  • Firefly Swarm: a cloud of tiny lights that flickers and orbits your cursor.

Everything is built from scratch in your brand’s colors, runs smoothly on phones, and calms itself down automatically for visitors who prefer less motion.
