How Does It Work? (It Doesn’t!)

We get this question constantly, mostly from ourselves at two in the morning. The honest answer is that it works the way a scenic overlook works: you arrive, you look at something pleasant, you take a photo, and then you leave without having achieved anything, feeling slightly better about everything. Here, the overlook is a web page, the scenery is a wave that never finishes breaking, and the photo is the screenshot you are definitely about to take.

In technical terms, which we will keep to a minimum because this is a page about nothing, a very patient computer draws a sky, a sea, and a boat, then draws them again, slightly to the side, sixty times every second, until you either leave or understand that this is, in fact, the whole show. The boat has not reached shore. The boat will never reach shore.5 The boat is doing great.

The real part

What you’re actually looking at

This page is a live demo of visual enhancements: animated touches that Cidron adds to a small business website so it looks polished and memorable.

Two moving backgrounds

The background slides sideways in an endless loop and swaps to a second one about every 26 seconds.

  • Clouds and Birds: layered clouds and flocks of birds drifting across a bright sky, with a slowly turning sun.
  • Rolling Waves: five layers of ocean waves rolling sideways with a little sailboat riding them.

A cursor effect that changes every 10 seconds

On a computer it follows your mouse. On a phone, touch the screen and drag. When nobody is touching anything it drifts on its own so you can watch it. The name of the current effect is shown at the bottom of the screen.

  • Emoji Parade: a little train of characters that follows your cursor.
  • Soap Bubbles: shimmering bubbles float up from your cursor.
  • Confetti Cannon: confetti pours from your cursor, with a big burst when you click or tap.
  • Ripple Rings: expanding rings, like raindrops on water.
  • Rainbow Ribbon: a wavy rainbow streamer behind your cursor.

Everything is built from scratch in your brand’s colors, runs smoothly on phones, and calms itself down automatically for visitors who prefer less motion.
